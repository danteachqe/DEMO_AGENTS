ENHANCED USER STORY
====================

As a bank customer with a valid debit/ATM card and PIN,
I want to withdraw cash from an ATM using my card and account,
So that I can have physical cash on hand without visiting a bank branch.

Technical Boundaries & Clarified Assumptions (resolved per DOR.MD ambiguity checks)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Authentication: User must insert/tap a valid card and enter a correct PIN (max 3 attempts
   before the card is retained/blocked).
2. Account selection: User selects the source account (Checking or Savings) when the card is
   linked to multiple accounts.
3. Withdrawal amount:
   - Must be entered in whole multiples of the smallest note denomination dispensed by the
     machine (e.g., multiples of $20).
   - Must not exceed the account's available balance.
   - Must not exceed the ATM's configured per-transaction and daily withdrawal limit.
4. Cash availability: The ATM must have sufficient physical cash/denominations to fulfill the
   request; otherwise the transaction is declined before the account is debited.
5. Network/session: The transaction requires a live connection to the bank's authorization
   system; if the network times out mid-transaction, the account must not be debited unless
   cash was confirmed dispensed (no double-debit, no free cash).
6. Confirmation & receipt: User is shown a confirmation screen with the amount and fee (if any)
   before the transaction is finalized, and is offered a printed or digital receipt.
7. Card return: The card is returned/ejected before or with the cash, per the ATM's
   configured card-return policy.
8. Session end: User is asked whether they want another transaction; if not (or after a
   timeout), the session ends and the screen returns to the idle/welcome state.
